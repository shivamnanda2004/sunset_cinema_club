Privacy Policy

Last updated 1 August 2026. This is a plain-language summary written for this demo site.

1. What we collect

When you book a ticket, join the club, or send us a message, we collect the details you provide directly — your name, email, phone number, and city. We also collect basic usage data, like which pages you visit, to keep the site working well.

2. How we use it

We use your details to confirm bookings, send updates about screenings you're attending, and — if you've opted in — let you know about new events. We don't sell your information to third parties.

3. Payment information

Ticket and gift card payments are processed by a third-party payment provider. We don't store your full card details on our servers.

4. Cookies

We use essential cookies to keep your city selection and cart working across pages, and optional analytics cookies to understand how the site is used.

5. Your choices

You can ask us to update or delete your information, or unsubscribe from emails, at any time by contacting contact@sunsetcinemaclub.in.

6. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we'll post the update here and adjust the "last updated" date at the top of the page.
