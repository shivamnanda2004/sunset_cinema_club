Our story

About Us

Sunset Cinema Club started with a simple complaint: the best part of going to the movies used to be the going out. We brought that back.

How we got here

We started with a single projector, a bedsheet screen, and a rooftop in one city. The idea was never complicated: take a film people already love, put it somewhere memorable, and let the setting do the rest.

A few years and a lot of extension cords later, we run screenings across five cities — on rooftops, beaches, brewery lawns, hotel gardens and amphitheatres — and we're still adding new venues every season.

Three things we won't compromise on

The setting matters

A film changes depending on where you watch it. We pick venues that add something a theatre seat can't.

Good sound, a real screen, comfortable seating — the basics, taken seriously every single time.

Everyone's welcome

Solo, in a group, on a first date, with your parents — our screenings are built for all of it.
